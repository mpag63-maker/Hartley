    How the projections work
    • Data: official Fantasy Premier League API — every finished gameweek's per-player stats (minutes, starts, xG, xA, goals, assists, bonus, saves, defensive contributions, cards), fixtures, injury flags and prices. Refreshed every 10 minutes.
    • Team strength: attack and defence ratings from each finished fixture (65% xG, 35% actual goals), weighting recent games more, adjusted for home advantage.
    • Players: start and 60-minute probabilities from the last six team fixtures; per-90 goal, assist, bonus, save and defensive-contribution rates (recency weighted, shrunk towards positional averages for small samples); scaled by the opponent and venue, and by the official chance-of-playing flag.
    • Scenarios: 10,000 simulated gameweeks of your squad — shared clean sheets between team-mates, FPL auto-sub rules, captain/vice — giving the worst case (10th percentile), base case (average) and best case (90th percentile). Transfer hits are deducted.
    • Transfers: every single, double and (extended) triple move is scored on weighted expected points over your planning horizon, respecting budget, selling prices and the 3-per-club rule. Rolling a transfer is valued at ~1.5 points.

    Projections are estimates, not guarantees — always check late team news before the deadline.
